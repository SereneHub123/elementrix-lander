CHAPTER I

General Conduct

RULE 1Treat Others Respectfully

Harassing, bullying, threatening, or repeatedly targeting other players is not allowed.

RULE 2Respect Staff

Disrespecting, insulting, threatening, or intentionally antagonizing Elementrix staff is not allowed. You may disagree with a staff decision, but do so respectfully and through the appropriate channels.

RULE 3Use Common Sense

Not every possible form of misconduct can be listed. If something is clearly intended to cause unnecessary harm, disruption, or abuse, staff may take action.

RULE 4Do Not Intentionally Disrupt the Server

Actions intended to seriously disrupt gameplay, events, communities, or server operations may result in punishment.

CHAPTER II

Griefing & Gameplay Interaction

RULE 5Griefing Is Allowed

Griefing is fully allowed on Elementrix, provided that it does not involve breaking any other server rule. Players are responsible for protecting their own builds, items, bases, and resources where applicable.

RULE 6Do Not Use Rule Violations to Grief

Griefing does not give you permission to harass players, exploit bugs, cheat, dox someone, or otherwise violate the rules.

CHAPTER III

Language & Communication

RULE 7Excessive Swearing Is Not Allowed

Swearing is permitted in moderation, but excessive or constant profanity is not allowed.

RULE 8No Harassment

Repeated insults, personal attacks, targeted abuse, or attempts to make another player uncomfortable are not allowed.

RULE 9Keep Chat Reasonable

Spam, excessive flooding, intentionally disruptive messages, and similar behavior may be moderated.

CHAPTER IV

Privacy & Personal Information

RULE 10No Doxing

Doxing is strictly prohibited. Do not leak, distribute, threaten to distribute, or encourage the sharing of another person's personal information. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Real names
  • Home addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Private social media accounts
  • School or workplace information
  • IP addresses
  • Private photographs
  • Other personally identifying or private information

Do not attempt to obtain or expose another player's private information.

CHAPTER V

Advertising & Promotion

RULE 11Advertising Is Restricted

Advertising or promoting outside servers, communities, products, services, social media pages, or other projects is not allowed without staff permission.

RULE 12Official Partner Exception

The only exception is Enzonic Cloud, Elementrix's official partner. Promotion of Enzonic Cloud is permitted where authorized by Elementrix. Attempting to disguise unauthorized advertising as normal conversation does not make it permitted.

CHAPTER VI

Exploits, Bugs & Duplication

RULE 13Do Not Exploit Bugs

Using glitches, unintended mechanics, vulnerabilities, or server bugs to gain an unfair advantage is not allowed.

RULE 14Duplication Is Not Allowed

Item duplication, currency duplication, resource duplication, or any similar exploit is strictly prohibited.

RULE 15Report Bugs Instead

If you discover a bug or exploit, report it to staff instead of abusing it. Elementrix may provide rewards for valid and responsibly reported bugs. Do not repeatedly reproduce, distribute, or teach others how to abuse a serious exploit.

RULE 16Exploit Abuse May Result in Rollbacks or Removal

Items, progress, currency, or other benefits obtained through exploits may be removed, rolled back, or otherwise invalidated.

CHAPTER VII

Cheats & Unfair Advantages

RULE 17No Cheats

Any cheat or modification that provides an unfair gameplay advantage is not allowed.

RULE 18No Hacked Clients

Hacked clients, cheat clients, unauthorized combat modifications, or similar tools that provide unfair advantages are prohibited.

RULE 19No Other Unfair Advantages

Do not use external tools, software, macros, automation, or modifications to gain an unfair advantage where prohibited by Elementrix gameplay rules.

RULE 20Do Not Assist Cheaters

Knowingly helping another player cheat, supplying cheats, or intentionally helping someone bypass anti-cheat systems may result in punishment.

CHAPTER VIII

Trading & Real-World Transactions

RULE 21No IRL Trading

Real-money trading (IRL trading) is not allowed. Do not buy, sell, or exchange in-game items, currency, accounts, ranks, services, or other Elementrix assets for real-world money unless explicitly authorized by Elementrix.

RULE 22No Off-Platform Deals

Attempting to arrange unauthorized real-money transactions through private messages or external platforms is also prohibited.

CHAPTER IX

Fair Play & Community Safety

RULE 23Do Not Impersonate Staff

Do not impersonate Elementrix staff members or falsely claim to have staff authority.

RULE 24Do Not Evade Punishments

Circumventing a punishment through alternate accounts or other methods may lead to additional action.

RULE 25Do Not Encourage Rule Breaking

Encouraging, organizing, or assisting others in breaking Elementrix rules may result in punishment, even if you did not directly perform the violation.

RULE 26Protect Your Account

You are responsible for keeping your account secure. Do not intentionally give other people access to your account and then attempt to blame staff for resulting losses.

CHAPTER X

Enforcement

RULE 27Staff Have Final Authority

Elementrix staff may investigate violations and take appropriate action based on the circumstances.

RULE 28Punishments May Vary

Punishments may include warnings, mutes, kicks, temporary bans, permanent bans, removals of illegitimate items or progress, or other appropriate action. The severity of a punishment may depend on:

  • The seriousness of the violation
  • Whether it was intentional
  • Previous offenses
  • The impact on other players or the server
  • Cooperation with staff

RULE 29Rule Loopholes Are Not Allowed

Attempting to exploit wording or loopholes in these rules to justify behavior that obviously violates their intent may still result in punishment.

RULE 30Common Sense Applies

These rules are not intended to cover every possible situation. If you have to ask whether something is obviously abusive, disruptive, or unfair, don't do it.

THE SPIRIT OF THE LAW.

Elementrix is built around freedom, competition, and player interaction. Griefing is allowed, but that freedom does not extend to harassment, cheating, exploiting, doxing, or anything that undermines the community. Play fairly, respect other players and staff, and use common sense.
